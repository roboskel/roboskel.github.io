Christos Spatharis acquired his B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Computer Science from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, University of Ioannina, in 2016 and 2018, respectively. In June 2023 he received his Ph.D. in Deep Reinforcement Learning and Generative Adversarial Modeling from the same department. During his academic career, he worked as a research associate in several European projects and participated in the publication of multiple research papers. His main research interests lie on the field of reinforcement learning, human-robot collaboration, imitation learning, pattern recognition, and multi-agent systems.
